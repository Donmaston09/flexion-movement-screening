What this page is. It runs the same pose model as Flexion for a short fixed period and records how it behaves on this device: model start-up, GPU or CPU, camera resolution, speed, how often a person and a whole body are found, and whether a second person or too little space is picked up. It does not score any movements, upload anything, or store any video, images or landmark coordinates. Use it with the device test protocol, then copy or download the results.
